# Decisions and ambiguities

1. **Source precedence:** Use the dated circular for clauses it explicitly amends/deletes; retain the master policy for clauses marked unchanged. The FAQ is older and is not treated as authoritative where it conflicts.
2. **UPI settlement:** Clause 1.1 is replaced from 1 Aug 2026: T+0 only with the active Instant Settlement add-on; otherwise T+1. The default answer does not assume the merchant has the add-on.
3. **MDR cap:** Circular clause C changes the cap to ₹15 for monthly volume up to ₹10 lakh and ₹25 above ₹10 lakh. GST at 18% is added to MDR and is outside the cap.
4. **MDR arithmetic:** Apply the percentage rate first, then the applicable cap, then calculate GST on the capped MDR. Round displayed money to two decimal places.
5. **Refund timing:** Before 1 Sep 2026, master clause 3.3 allows full MDR reversal within 24 hours. Starting 1 Sep 2026, the circular deletes 3.3 and says clause 3.2 applies without exception.
6. **Daily limit:** The circular changes clause 4.3 to ₹1,00,000 or the customer's own bank limit, whichever is lower, across all UPI transactions. Therefore two ₹1,00,000 payments in one day are not allowed under this limit.
7. **Account closure:** The policy requires 30 days' written notice; pending settlements are released within 10 working days of closure after dues. “I closed it yesterday” is not enough to calculate a calendar release date.
8. **Citations:** Cite only clause labels that appear in the source documents. The FAQ uses question headings rather than numbered clauses, so cite the FAQ heading instead of inventing a clause number.
9. **Unclear merchant tier/amount:** For MDR calculations, ask for missing transaction amount or monthly volume tier instead of guessing.
10. **Scope:** Unsupported questions return CANNOT_ANSWER. This intentionally favors grounded abstention over inventing policy.
